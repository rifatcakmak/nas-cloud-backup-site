Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

NAS Cloud Backup is a personal backup workflow operated solely by its owner. Its Google OAuth authorization allows the owner to write, list, read, and restore encrypted backup files in the owner's Google Drive account.

Data handling

Backup content is encrypted locally using Restic before upload with Rclone. The website does not receive or store backed-up photos, videos, Google account data, passwords, or access tokens. The owner controls the backup repository and its retention.

Sharing

The application does not sell personal data or intentionally share backup data with third parties other than the storage provider selected by the owner. Google processes stored files subject to its own policies.

Retention and deletion

The owner can manage and delete backup data through the backup software and Google Drive. Encrypted copies can remain until explicitly removed or removed through an owner-configured retention process.

Website

This informational website has no analytics code, advertising trackers, or forms. The hosting platform may collect technical access logs under its own terms.

Contact

Contact the operator via GitHub Issues.
